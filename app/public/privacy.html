Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 5, 2026

ClariTale turns AI concepts and research papers into interactive explainer pages. This page explains what we store about you, why, and how to delete it. We keep it short and plain.

What we store

  • Your account: your email address and, if you sign in with Google, your Google account ID. We ask Google only for your name, email and basic profile (the openid, email and profile permissions). We don't get access to your Gmail, Drive, contacts or anything else.
  • Your explainers: the topics you type, the papers you upload, and the pages, chats and settings that come from them, so your history is there when you come back.
  • Usage: how many explainers you make and what the AI calls cost, so we can apply the free daily limits. Sign-in sessions store your browser's user agent and IP address, to keep your account safe and limit abuse.
  • Preferences: any preferences you choose to save from feedback.

How we use it

Only to run ClariTale: to sign you in, make and save your explainers, apply the free limits and prevent abuse. We don't sell your data, show ads, or share it with anyone for marketing.

Who else processes it

  • Anthropic: the text of your topics and papers is sent to Anthropic's API to write the explainer pages and answer your questions.
  • Google: handles "Continue with Google" sign-in.
  • Render: hosts the app and its database.

Cookies

We use only the cookies sign-in needs: a session cookie that keeps you signed in, and a short-lived cookie during Google sign-in. No tracking or advertising cookies.

Deleting your data

You can delete your account at any time from Account → Delete account. This permanently deletes your account, explainers, chats and preferences. We keep cost records of the AI calls you made, with a one-way scrambled form of your email instead of the address itself (so deleting and re-creating an account can't reset the daily free limit) and the network you used. These hold no topic or page content.
